About ScotMortgages & Financial Services

Formed and based in Dundee in 1997 under the banner of RWS Financial Consultants the business moved to Cupar in 2002 and then in 2023 moved to our present, more client friendly premises at Westrigg, West Port, Cupar under the new trading name of ScotMortgages & Financial Services. All of our Advisers are now based here, covering the whole of Scotland giving face to face advice in clients own homes, places of work or in our office.

We have an experienced team focused on delivery of service to our clients, who all share the same enthusiasm and dedication. In turn they are supported by technical product specialists at Openwork, one of the UK's largest advice networks.
